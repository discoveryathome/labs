## Before opening donated sessions

1. A consenting scientific maintainer declares review capacity and credit terms.
2. An independent reviewer checks candidate arithmetic, provenance and novelty.
3. Shared claims and handoffs are configured and the hub assesses readiness.
4. A new bounded trial tests whether further structured improvements remain
   accessible after accounting for these already discovered candidates.

The current evidence supports a focused structured-search project. It does not
establish a sustainable rate of easy records for successive short sessions.
